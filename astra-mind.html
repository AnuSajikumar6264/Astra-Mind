Simulated crew and readings only, tagged SYNTHETIC. Anomaly scores are statistical deviations from each person’s own baseline (robust z-score over the first 72 hours), not disease probabilities. The forecast is a damped-trend Holt model with a 90% interval, a lightweight stand-in for Chronos-2. Every finding needs medical review.
